## The Problem We Solve

ADHD kids struggle with three things every single week:
1. **Remembering** what was assigned
2. **Communicating** it accurately to parents
3. **Starting** it once they know what it is

Parents are exhausted from fighting all three battles. Not once. Not twice. Week after week. Year after year.

## Core Brand Positioning

### Primary Tagline
> *The system isn't broken. It just wasn't built with your child in mind.*

**Why this works:**
- Removes blame from the child ("they're not the problem")
- Removes blame from the school ("the system isn't broken")
- Validates the parent ("you're not imagining it")
- Opens the door ("there's something built with your child in mind")
- Non-confrontational — suitable for school partnership conversations

### Secondary Tagline (hero message / conversion)
> *Stop asking "what homework do you have?" FocablyED already knows.*

**Where to use:** Landing page hero, paid ads, App Store short description context.

### Original tagline (still valid, use sparingly)
> *Wrong system. Not wrong kid.*

More punchy, slightly more confrontational. Good for social/awareness. Less suitable for school-facing contexts.

## The Two-Audience Funnel

### Audience 1 — Parent (downloads the app, pays)
- Exhausted. Has tried everything — whiteboards, paper planners, reminder apps, nagging, reward charts.
- Not looking for another tool for their kid.
- Looking for **a window into what's actually happening** between their child and the teacher.
- Language that resonates: *time blindness, task initiation, executive function, working memory, silent oversight, transition struggles*
- The sell: visibility and peace of mind, not features.

### Audience 2 — Child (uses the app daily)
- ADHD kids (including teens) often like cute/playful things — don't over-age the UI.
- Squirrel mascot works once mum or dad has already converted.
- The sell inside the app: XP, rewards, Squirrel breaking things down into small steps.

**Key insight:** The parent is the customer. The child is the user. The teacher is the distribution channel.

## Squirrel — The Mascot

### Origin story
Named after the ADHD distraction reflex — "SQUIRREL!" from Pixar's UP. Steve's daughter Zoe (15, ADHD) gets distracted like Doug the dog. The name is an in-joke that every ADHD family immediately gets.

### Why it works across ages
- For parents: "SQUIRREL!" is a knowing wink. An in-joke that signals "we get it."
- For kids: cute squirrel character with personality. ADHD kids (including teens) engage with this more than expected.
- Not childish — it's purposeful. Squirrel knows something most people don't: big things grow from single acorns.

### The acorn parable
> *Squirrel knows something most kids don't — big things grow from single acorns. One step. One task. One win.*

Squirrel's whole philosophy maps onto ADHD: the problem isn't capability, it's starting. An acorn becomes an oak. One task becomes a habit. Small wins build into something bigger.

### Squirrel's gender
Referred to as "she" throughout the app.

## Freemium vs Pro — Value Proposition

### The critical insight
Parents who find FocablyED have already tried lists, reminders, and every other tool. They don't need another tool for their kid. The upgrade sell is removing the **last remaining friction point** — the information extraction problem.

### Freemium — "Finally a tool that helps"
> *Getting started is the hardest part for ADHD kids. FocablyED breaks any assignment into steps small enough to actually start.*

**What it solves:** Battle 3 (starting the work) once they have the information.
**What it doesn't solve:** The parent still has to wrestle assignment details out of their child.

### Family Pro — "We already know"
> *When their teacher connects to FocablyED, assignments arrive automatically — before your child even gets home. No more "I don't know." No more scrambling at 9pm.*

**What it solves:** All three battles. Assignment info arrives automatically. Squirrel starts breaking it down. Parent sees everything silently in real time.

### The upgrade moment
> *Your child's teacher is already in FocablyED. Upgrade to see everything they're assigning and whether your kid is actually doing it.*

### Teacher access
- **Teachers are always free.** Always.
- Teachers are the distribution channel, not the customer.
- One teacher brings 25-30 families.
- Parent pays to **connect** to the teacher and receive their assignments.

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1 — 50 paying families (before school sales)
- Parent-first, founder-led, organic
- No paid ads yet
- No active school outreach yet

### Channel — Facebook (primary conversion channel)
- Join ADHD parent groups as **Steve** (not as FocablyED brand)
- Key groups: ADHD Parents Australia, Raising Children with ADHD Australia, state-based groups
- Participate genuinely for 2-3 weeks before mentioning the product
- Respond to every comment personally

### Founder post sequence

**Post 1 — Problem (no product mention)**
```
My daughter has ADHD. She's 15.

Every night I ask "do you have any homework?" Every night the answer is "no."

Until one night it's "yes, it's due tomorrow and I haven't started."
Or worse — an email from the teacher saying she missed the deadline.

She wants to do it. She just can't get started.
And by the time I find out — it's already too late.

I kept thinking at 15 she'd start to own it herself.
That the accountability would just... click.

Still waiting.

Same argument. Same tears. Week after week, year after year.

How did you manage this?
```

**Post 2 — Solution (after comments roll in, ~day 2)**
Respond to every comment genuinely first. Then follow-up post reveals the app — built by a dad, for families like yours. Kim's role as the person who lived it daily can be woven in here.

### Why Facebook over Instagram
- Facebook = **conversion** (parents, purchase decisions, community groups)
- Instagram = **awareness** (younger audience, brand building, longer term)
- Start Facebook. Add Instagram later.

### Metric that matters
**50 paying families** — not follower count, not app downloads.

## Competitive Positioning

### What makes FocablyED uncontested
The **three-way accountability loop** — student + parent + teacher — closed in one place. No other tool at scale in Australia does this cleanly for ADHD families.

### What competitors offer
- Reminder apps: solve nothing for ADHD kids (they ignore reminders)
- School management systems (Compass, Sentral): teacher/admin facing, not parent/student UX
- ClassDojo: classroom behaviour, not homework accountability
- General task apps: no parent visibility, no teacher connection

### Potential acquirers (when the time comes)
Compass Education (most likely), Sentral, ClassDojo, Edrolo.

### The moat
**ADHD community.** A platform with a community beats a tool with ADHD features. The Facebook group strategy builds this from day one.
